# Security

Please report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** button on the
[Security tab](https://github.com/ez2app/ez2-aib/security) of this repository. Do not open a public issue for them.

We will acknowledge your report, keep you updated, and credit you in the release notes unless you ask us not to.
